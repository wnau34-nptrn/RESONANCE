# 1. THE NATURE OF CALDRIS

Caldris is not a world divided into four elemental kingdoms.

Beacon, Glasswind, Fracture, and Wildroot are not elements.

They are four different ways reality can remain coherent.

The world does not ask where a creature was born.

It asks what that creature is doing.

This is why a Wildroot creature may fulfill a Beacon Journey by entering combat and surviving.  
A Beacon creature may fulfill a Wildroot Journey by becoming GROWN.  
A Glasswind creature may die and resonate with Fracture.

Resonance is not nationality.

Resonance is **meaning expressed through action**.

---

# 2. WHAT A CARD REPRESENTS

A Keeper does not literally summon dead historical figures from paper cards.

Keepers practice a phenomenon known as:

## Resonant Manifestation

Every important event, creature, object, place, ritual, and decision leaves an imprint in Resonance.

These imprints are called:

## Echoes

A sufficiently attuned Keeper can manifest an Echo temporarily into the present.

Therefore:

- **Creature Card** = Echo of a real creature, person, order, or archetypal being.
- **Artifact** = Resonant imprint of a persistent relic, engine, structure, or sacred object.
- **Equipment** = Echo of a real weapon, armor, tool, or bound relic.
- **Sorcery** = Re-enactment of a real ritual, technique, doctrine, or historical event.

This also explains physical-card identity.

When a Keeper RETURNs and REPLAYs the same physical Creature, they are following the same **Echo-thread**, not summoning an unrelated duplicate.

# 3. THE FIRST RESONANCE

Long before humans, before nations, before recorded history, there was only one coherent law beneath reality.

Later civilizations called it:

# The First Resonance

It was not a sound.

It was not magic.

It was not a god.

It was the condition that allowed existence itself to remain one continuous reality.

Life and death were not yet philosophically separate.

Memory and change were not enemies.

Growth and destruction were two phases of one motion.

Then something happened.

The First Resonance fractured.

From that rupture emerged four dominant truths:

- **Beacon — What must endure**
- **Glasswind — What must change**
- **Fracture — What must be paid**
- **Wildroot — What must continue through life**

Later people would call them the Four Resonances.

No surviving record proves whether the First Resonance broke naturally, was broken deliberately, or was never truly singular at all.

---

# 4. THE FOUR RESONANCES

## BEACON
### “What must endure.”

Beacon represents continuity.

Names.  
Promises.  
Formation.  
Memory.  
Civilization.  
Those who remain standing when something tries to erase them.

Beacon philosophy does not define victory as destroying an enemy.

Its deepest proof is:

> **“I entered conflict, and I am still here.”**

That is why Beacon Journey is fulfilled when an allied Creature attacks another Creature, Combat resolves, and the attacker remains alive in the Arena.

---

## GLASSWIND
### “Nothing truly stands still.”

Glasswind views reality as a network of possible routes.

Distance is negotiable.  
Sequence is negotiable.  
Presence itself may be temporary.

A departure is meaningless unless it creates a new return.

Thus Glasswind Journey requires:

> RETURN from Arena to hand, then replay the same physical being during the same Turn.

Glasswind does not worship speed.

It worships **possibility**.

---

## FRACTURE
### “Every future has a price.”

Fracture is widely feared and often misunderstood.

It does not worship death.

It believes that all futures are built from something that ceased to exist.

Cities replace ruins.  
New laws replace broken oaths.  
Survival sometimes requires sacrifice.

Its danger is moral, not merely magical.

Once someone believes loss is necessary, the next question becomes:

> **Who has the right to decide what must be lost?**

Fracture Journey is therefore triggered by true allied Death during the Keeper’s own Turn.

---

## WILDROOT
### “Life remembers.”

Wildroot is not merely nature.

It is inheritance.

Seeds know how to become trees without instruction.  
Roots return to ancient watercourses long after cities vanish.  
Bodies feed soil that feeds life that never knew them.

To Wildroot, growth is memory becoming form.

A Creature becoming GROWN represents a living being realizing a deeper possibility already carried within itself.

# 5. THE FIRST AGE
## THE AGE BEFORE NAMES

The earliest known civilization is called:

# The Asteri

No confirmed record explains where they came from.

Their oldest ruins appear across distant regions at nearly the same historical moment.

Asteri statues have no faces.

Their surviving inscriptions contain no direct equivalent of “king.”

Every major Asteri settlement contained structures now classified as:

# Landmarks

Landmarks were not monuments.

They were **Anchors of Reality**.

Their purpose was to preserve the memory of what a place truly was.

If the world changed, the Landmark could remind reality of an earlier state.

This is the origin of the word:

# Restore

Restore does not mean healing.

Restore means:

> **Forcing a place to remember what it once was.**

---

# 6. THE AGE OF FOUR PATHS

As the First Resonance became increasingly unstable, different groups of Asteri and later peoples began perceiving reality in distinct ways.

These traditions became Beacon, Glasswind, Fracture, and Wildroot.

At first they were not enemies.

This period became one of Caldris’s greatest ages.

Beacon preserved cities.

Glasswind linked distant regions.

Wildroot sustained ecosystems and food systems.

Fracture removed failing systems so that stronger ones could replace them.

During this age, people discovered that one mind could resonate with more than one path.

The practice became known as:

# Attune

And those rare individuals capable of maintaining multiple Resonances without losing themselves became known as:

# Keepers

---

# 7. PROJECT CONCORDANCE

Centuries of philosophical disagreement eventually convinced part of Asteri civilization that the Four Resonances were evidence of a broken world.

They began a project intended to reunify reality.

Its name was:

# THE CONCORDANCE

Public doctrine claimed that Concordance would end conflict by returning all Resonance to one perfect state.

But fragments of the Living Atlas suggest another objective.

The Asteri may have attempted not merely to repair the world—

but to **rewrite it**.

Landmarks across Caldris were incorporated into the Concordance network.

Starfall Observatory monitored something beyond the sky.

Whisperglass Relay linked distant nodes.

Forge of Echoes reconstructed prior states.

The exact function of the full network is unknown.

---

# 8. THE GREAT SUNDERING

When Concordance activated, the world did not reunify.

It broke.

The event is remembered as:

# THE GREAT SUNDERING

The sky opened into impossible geometric fractures.

Cities vanished.

Mountains appeared where seas had existed.

Rivers crossed themselves.

Some people reached home before they departed.

Forests grew through cities in a single night.

Some dead returned without memory of their former names.

Glasswind routes folded across centuries.

Fragments of Resonance embedded into living beings.

Landmarks overloaded.

Caldris survived.

But it was no longer one completely coherent world.

The central wound left by the catastrophe became:

# The Shattered Meridian

At its center lies a region where no known Resonance can be detected.

The Living Atlas marks it with a single word:

# ZERO

# 9. KEEPERS, ATTUNE, JOURNEY, AND RESTORE

## Keeper

A Keeper is someone capable of carrying multiple Resonant alignments without collapsing into one philosophy.

This is why deck construction may cross Resonances.

A Keeper is not defined by loyalty to one faction.

They are defined by the ability to hold contradiction.

---

## Attune

Attune is the conscious binding of one’s spirit to a Resonance pattern.

The first commitments are easier.

Deeper requirements demand increasing philosophical and spiritual alignment.

Attuning does not merely provide “mana.”

It changes what reality around the Keeper is willing to permit.

---

## Journey

Journey is the world recognizing that a Keeper has acted in accordance with a Resonance’s truth.

- Beacon: survive conflict.
- Glasswind: return and re-enter.
- Fracture: pay through true loss.
- Wildroot: become more than one was.

---

## Restore

Restore is Landmark progress.

A Keeper is not repairing stone.

They are reconstructing a Landmark’s **memory of itself**.

Only one Journey Restore may be gained per Own Turn because a Landmark does not respond to raw repetition.

It responds to a meaningful proof of intent.

# 11. BEACON
## THE DAWNLINE COMPACT

After the Sundering, Beacon survivors did not begin by creating an empire.

They built lights.

One tower saw the next.

The next saw another city.

Eventually a chain of illumination crossed the northern continent.

This became:

# The Dawnline

Its founding oath:

> **“So long as one tower can see another, no city shall disappear unwitnessed.”**

---

## AUREL VEYR
### The Dawnline Commander

Aurel Veyr was not a king.

He was not originally even the highest-ranking officer at Dawnline.

During the Sundering he refused evacuation orders and held the city’s defensive line for three days.

More than eighty thousand civilians escaped.

No confirmed body was ever found.

Centuries later, Living Atlas fragments still display a moving coordinate tagged:

**AUREL VEYR**

---

## CAEDRAN SOL
### The Sunspire Marshal

Caedran Sol was a strategist and Aurel’s closest companion.

Together they developed the military doctrine called:

# Shared Line

Its principle was simple:

> A soldier need not be invincible if the person beside them remains standing.

Caedran supported Concordance.

Aurel opposed it.

Their final meeting occurred one night before the Sundering.

No record survives of what they said.

Caedran’s weapon was later found at Dawnline.

Aurel was not.

---

# 12. GLASSWIND
## THOSE WHO REFUSE ONE ROAD

Glasswind maintains no permanent capital.

Many Glasswind societies consider the concept philosophically suspicious.

Their most valuable maps do not show where places are.

They show where places **could be**.

---

## SERIS VALE
### The Glasswind Wayfarer

Seris Vale became the first known person to enter a major Glasswind Rift without a Relay.

For her, the journey lasted three days.

When she emerged—

317 years had passed.

She had not aged.

Her first question was:

> “Has Concordance been activated?”

When told the Sundering occurred more than three centuries before, she replied:

> **“Then we still have time.”**

---

## NYM AVAR
### Riftwake Navigator

Nym was Seris Vale’s final known student before her disappearance.

According to history, he should have died centuries ago.

Yet modern explorers discovered his handwriting inside the Hall of Returning Stars.

The inscription was eleven days old.

Even stranger:

The Fractured Host hears a voice inside himself that claims to be Nym.

Seris insists the voice is not the Nym she knew.

---

# 13. FRACTURE
## THE PRICE OF TOMORROW

Fracture is the Resonance most commonly described as evil.

That judgment is too simple.

But it is not entirely unfair.

Fracture’s philosophy can justify terrible acts if one person decides that another person’s suffering is necessary.

---

## VARKESH THANE
### The Bloodwake Conductor

Varkesh once served Beacon command under Caedran Sol.

During the war after the Sundering, the city of Kharad Vale was judged strategically unsalvageable.

High Command withdrew.

Varkesh remained.

Seven defenders survived.

He later learned that the city’s destruction had delayed the enemy long enough for Sunspire to survive.

That day Varkesh abandoned Beacon doctrine.

He founded:

# The Bloodwake Covenant

Its first law:

> **“Those who order another to die must stand near enough to hear it.”**

---

## KING MORDREN IX
### The Thronebound Revenant

Mordren ruled Vhal-Turog when Resonance collapse threatened the city.

He ordered Crackseers to bind the city’s survival to his own Spirit.

The ritual worked.

In one sense.

Mordren survived.

His throne survived.

His palace survived.

The citizens did not.

More than three centuries later he still sits on his throne, issuing decrees to a nation that no longer exists.

---

# 14. WILDROOT
## THE MEMORY BENEATH THE SOIL

Wildroot cultures do not keep history primarily in books.

They believe removing memory from living systems makes memory incomplete.

History is stored in groves.

Burn scars record wars.

Growth rings record climate.

Root direction records migration.

Some forests remember events older than civilization.

---

## ORUN-KAEL
### Ancient Worldbark

No one knows whether Orun-Kael is truly a tree.

It has roots.

It has leaves.

It grows.

But constellations carved naturally into its bark do not exist in the current sky.

Rootkeepers believe Orun-Kael predates the Sundering.

Living Atlas data suggests something more disturbing.

It may predate the Asteri.

When Seris Vale returned after 317 years, she recognized Orun-Kael.

History records no meeting between them.

---

## THE WORLDROOT SLEEPER

Wildroot possesses no true name for the entity humans call the Worldroot Sleeper.

To name something in old Rootkeeper tradition means claiming to understand it.

No Rootkeeper claims to understand the Sleeper.

Its root network extends beneath nearly one third of the continent.

One theory claims the forests do not grow above the Worldroot Sleeper.

The forests are part of its body.

# 15. THE SIX GREAT LANDMARKS

## 15.1 WHISPERGLASS RELAY

Location: Glassward Coast, near the Rift Sea.

Once part of a continent-wide transportation system.

Every Relay failed during the Sundering except Whisperglass.

No one knows why.

Its awakened memory produces the principle remembered as:

### Echo Assault

Ancient Glasswind soldiers stationed here were once ordered to continue moving even after conventional command had collapsed.

---

## 15.2 MOONBRIDGE CROSSING

Location: across the Shattered Meridian.

Part of the bridge does not physically exist in the current world.

Travelers sometimes cross distances they never actually traversed.

Its restored memory can refund Resonant expenditure because, from one timeline’s perspective, part of the journey never occurred.

---

## 15.3 STARFALL OBSERVATORY

Location: Astral Reach, far north.

The Observatory was not built to observe stars in general.

Every primary instrument targets the same region of apparently empty sky.

Asteri records call it:

# The Dark Interval

The last surviving observation reads:

> “It is not approaching us. We are approaching it.”

Its awakened memory manifests as:

### Foresee

The ability to perceive several possible paths and choose one.

---

## 15.4 STILLWATER SANCTUARY

Location: Stillwater Basin, southwest of the Meridian.

Its lake remains perfectly calm even during storms.

The water does not reflect a being as they currently are.

It reflects the most complete condition they once possessed.

This is the origin of its restorative effect.

---

## 15.5 FORGE OF ECHOES

Location: Ashen Marches near the Meridian.

The Forge has no conventional fire.

It uses remembered states as heat.

Asteri smiths could reconstruct an object toward a prior ideal condition.

Its Landmark effect strengthens the next arriving Creature by imposing the memory of a stronger possible state.

---

## 15.6 HALL OF RETURNING STARS

Location: Variable.

The Hall has no stable geographic coordinates.

Living Atlas has mapped it:

- in the Meridian,
- at sea,
- inside mountain systems,
- and once thousands of kilometers above Starfall Observatory.

Thousands entered the Hall during the Sundering.

Only thirteen were known to exit.

Each emerged in a different historical era.

Its memory causes arrivals to occur sooner than ordinary reality expects.

> **Note:** The Core Signature Artifact **Hall of Returning Steps** is a specific inner passage / ritual architecture associated with the wider Landmark known as the **Hall of Returning Stars**. The two names are intentionally distinct.

# 16. THE GREAT CHARACTER WEB

## Aurel Veyr ↔ Caedran Sol

Brothers in arms.

Architects of Shared Line.

Later enemies in philosophy.

Caedran believed Concordance could end future wars.

Aurel believed:

> **“Difference is not damage that must be repaired.”**

---

## Caedran Sol ↔ Varkesh Thane

Varkesh once admired Caedran more than his own father.

That is why Kharad Vale broke him so completely.

To Varkesh, Beacon preached universal protection while quietly deciding some people were strategically disposable.

His Fracture philosophy grew from disillusionment with Beacon hypocrisy.

---

## Seris Vale ↔ Aurel Veyr

A Living Atlas fragment claims Seris met Aurel seventeen years after the Sundering.

Seris denies ever meeting him.

Yet when shown his portrait, she identified him immediately.

---

## Nym Avar ↔ The Fractured Host

One of the voices within the Host claims to be Nym.

Seris says it is not him.

Possible explanations include:

- another Nym from another timeline,
- an Echo with corrupted identity,
- a false intelligence using Nym’s memory,
- or something older wearing his name.

---

## Mordren IX ↔ Orun-Kael

Vhal-Turog once attempted to burn part of the First Grove.

Orun-Kael remembers every soldier who entered.

When asked why it did not kill Mordren after the Sundering, it replied:

> **“Death is a mercy he has not yet learned to request.”**

# 17. LIORA
## KEEPER OF THE ATLAS

Liora is not a chosen one.

She has no divine bloodline.

She is an explorer of Asteri ruins.

During an expedition she discovered a Living Atlas Fragment.

When she touched it, she saw thousands of overlapping maps of Caldris.

In one:

Beacon ruled the world.

In another:

Wildroot consumed all cities.

In another:

The Sundering never happened.

And in one:

Caldris did not exist.

Afterward, the Fragment began identifying awakening Landmarks.

Each awakening was accompanied by the same word:

# CONVERGENCE

Liora travels not to dominate the Landmarks, but to understand why they are waking.

Her mixed-Resonance deck reflects her narrative role.

She slowly discovers that no single Resonance possesses the whole truth.

---

# 18. THE FRACTURED HOST

No reliable record preserves his original name.

His body contains multiple embedded Resonance fragments.

Each contains memories.

Some are from people who died during the Sundering.

Some appear older than the Sundering.

Some may not belong to this version of Caldris at all.

He constantly hears voices.

One of them knows more about the First Resonance than any Living Atlas fragment.

The Fractured Host also seeks to Restore Landmarks.

But unlike Liora, he believes he already understands their purpose.

He claims:

> **“The Landmarks were never built to save Caldris.”**

> **“They are pieces of the machine that broke it.”**

# 19. CALDRIS — WORLD GEOGRAPHY

Caldris surrounds a massive central wound called:

# The Shattered Meridian

From above, the Meridian resembles a broken ring radiating instability through the continent.

Its major regions:

| Region | Location | Dominant Influence | Major Sites |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Dawn Crown | North / Northeast | Beacon | Sunspire, Azure Gate, Dawnline Citadel |
| Glassward Coast | West | Glasswind | Whisperglass Relay, Rift Sea, Wayfarer ports |
| Astral Reach | Far North | Asteri / Glasswind | Starfall Observatory |
| Ashen Marches | East | Fracture | Vhal-Turog, Kharad Vale, Forge of Echoes |
| Verdant Deep | South | Wildroot | First Grove, Orun-Kael, Worldroot basin |

| Shattered Meridian | Center | Unstable / Mixed | Concordance ruins, Moonbridge Crossing |

These are not four color-coded nations.

Resonance influence overlaps.

Old Beacon cities exist in present Fracture territory.

Wildroot and Beacon jointly protect certain Sanctuaries.

Glasswind routes cross all borders.

# 25. HIDDEN TRUTHS

The Lore should preserve three layers of historical truth.

## OFFICIAL HISTORY

Concordance failed.

The failure caused the Great Sundering.

## FORBIDDEN HISTORY

Concordance had been attempted before.

The earlier attempt discovered Zero Resonance.

## TRUE HISTORY — NOT YET FULLY REVEALED

Concordance may not have been designed primarily to unite the Four Resonances.

It may have been designed to close something.

The Sundering may have occurred because some Asteri deliberately destroyed their own system to stop an external presence.

Thus the event remembered as history’s greatest catastrophe may also have been:

# The Asteri’s final victory.

## THE ELSEWHERE

After the Seventh Silence, routes may open to alternate Caldris realities created by different Sundering outcomes.

Beacon became the dominant civilizing force.

### A Caldris where Fracture became the primary philosophy

Sacrifice is openly acknowledged.

### A Caldris where Seris never returned

The major question becomes:

> **Is a painful world with choice better than a perfect world without freedom?**

Each Resonance answers differently.

# FINAL LORE PRINCIPLE

WONDERBOUND: RESONANCE should not be a story about four colored factions fighting because they are different.

It is a story about four incomplete truths.

Beacon is correct that some things must endure.

Glasswind is correct that nothing should remain trapped forever.

Fracture is correct that futures demand a price.

Wildroot is correct that life survives by becoming something new.

The catastrophe begins whenever one truth claims it is sufficient by itself.

And the role of the Keeper is not to choose the “correct” Resonance.

It is to survive the contradiction between them.

> **The world was not broken because it contained four truths.**
>
> **It was broken because someone tried to force them back into one.**

# 1. Production Goal

The Core Set must look as though every card was photographed or painted from the **same historical world**, even when the cards depict different centuries, factions, rituals, creatures, and regions.

Production must preserve three things simultaneously:

1. **Immediate card readability** — subject and action identifiable at Hand, Board, Detail, Permanent, and Deck crops.
2. **World continuity** — repeated characters, orders, architecture, relics, and Resonance motifs remain visually consistent.
3. **Narrative specificity** — an image should depict *this* card from Caldris, not a generic fantasy substitute.

### Core technical standard

- Master: **2048 × 1152 px / 16:9 landscape**.
- No baked text, typography, card frames, UI, icons, cost badges, AT/HP, or rules symbols.
- Critical identity stays within the central safe area.
- Creature art prioritizes head / upper-body identity for Board crop.
- Artifact / Equipment art keeps the primary object fully readable at Permanent thumbnail size.
- Sorcery art centers the actual action or consequence.
- Per-card focal override is an exception, not the normal workflow.

# 2. Shared Caldris Art Direction

**Original style target:** cinematic dark high fantasy with painterly realism, readable silhouettes, atmospheric depth, ancient material culture, and restrained supernatural light. The goal is epic and mythic without copying the visual identity of any existing franchise.

### Shared world anchors

- **Asteri:** faceless monumental sculpture, pale mineral stone, black glass, aged bronze, mathematically calm geometry, structures that imply technology without modern machinery.
- **Resonance light:** behaves like a physical property of matter, not a generic spell glow.
- **Age:** almost everything important has history—wear, repair, mineral staining, root intrusion, soot, salt, or weathering.
- **Scale:** heroic characters remain human-sized; true world-scale entities and architecture must be shown with environmental scale cues.
- **Magic:** should be legible from cause and effect. Avoid random particles when a concrete visual metaphor can explain the mechanic.

### Global avoid list

- modern firearms, modern tactical clothing, cyberpunk UI, holographic screens
- generic MMORPG armor clutter
- direct visual imitation of existing franchises
- excessive bloom / neon saturation
- readable modern alphabets or fake text as decoration
- gore or graphic body horror
- meaningless floating runes everywhere

# 3. Four Resonance Visual Languages
## BEACON

**Core thesis:** Continuity, formation, memory, disciplined protection.

**Shape language:** vertical towers, nested arches, radial sun-lines, concentric shields, ordered repetition.
**Materials:** pale stone, ivory enamel, brushed brass, warm lightglass, woven command banners.
**Lighting:** dawn-gold and warm white key light with deep teal-blue environmental shadows.
**Architecture:** monumental terraces, relay towers, elevated bridges, disciplined defensive geometry.
**Motion language:** forward pressure, braced stance, linked formations; never frantic.
**Must avoid:** angel wings, real-world religious symbols, generic holy-paladin clichés, excessive yellow glow, spotless utopia.

**Prompt anchor:** `Beacon visual language: monumental disciplined fantasy, pale stone and brushed brass, warm lightglass, dawn-gold rim light, nested arches and ordered formations`

## GLASSWIND

**Core thesis:** Possibility, motion, return, routes that do not obey ordinary space.

**Shape language:** offset frames, sweeping diagonals, mirrored arcs, split horizons, impossible perspective.
**Materials:** smoked glass, mirrorsteel, silvered crystal, translucent silk, weathered blue-gray leather.
**Lighting:** cool moonlight, cyan-silver edges, restrained violet refraction, prismatic highlights.
**Architecture:** bridges with missing spans, suspended walkways, reflective towers, mobile ports, impossible corridors.
**Motion language:** mid-step, wind-driven cloth, duplicate echoes, directional flow.
**Must avoid:** cyberpunk neon, sci-fi hologram UI, generic portal circles everywhere, weightless clutter.

**Prompt anchor:** `Glasswind visual language: elegant impossible-space fantasy, smoked glass and mirrorsteel, cool moonlit cyan-silver refraction, moving cloth, offset paths and mirrored architecture`

## FRACTURE

**Core thesis:** Cost, consequence, controlled collapse, power released through breaking.

**Shape language:** split circles, stress lines, wedges, asymmetry, cracked planes, interrupted symmetry.
**Materials:** blackened iron, obsidian, cracked ceramic, ash-stained cloth, dark red resin-like fracture light.
**Lighting:** low ember-red and bruised magenta accents against heavy shadow; light emerges from breaks.
**Architecture:** broken fortresses, half-collapsed thrones, scorched causeways, ritual chambers under tension.
**Motion language:** impact, recoil, rupture, deliberate sacrifice; physical weight matters.
**Must avoid:** gore, exposed organs, generic demons, real-world occult symbols, gratuitous skull decoration, horror-for-horror's-sake.

**Prompt anchor:** `Fracture visual language: severe dark fantasy of controlled rupture, blackened iron and obsidian, ember-red fracture light, asymmetrical stressed geometry, heavy physical consequence without gore`

## WILDROOT

**Core thesis:** Inheritance, growth, ecosystems, memory carried through living matter.

**Shape language:** branching spirals, growth rings, root networks, overlapping organic layers, asymmetrical living silhouettes.
**Materials:** ancient bark, mossed stone, amber sap, living wood, seed husks, muted bioluminescent fungi.
**Lighting:** filtered green-gold canopy light, amber sap glow, deep humid shadows, occasional pale moonlight.
**Architecture:** groves grown around ruins, root bridges, living halls, canopy sanctuaries, structures shaped rather than built.
**Motion language:** slow expansion, curling roots, falling seeds, breathing foliage, immense biological weight.
**Must avoid:** generic druid robes, cute forest-fairy look, oversaturated neon green, random vines on everything, cartoon nature motifs.

**Prompt anchor:** `Wildroot visual language: ancient living high fantasy, bark and mossed stone, amber sap light, layered roots and growth rings, humid atmospheric forest depth, organic but monumental`

# 4. Cross-Resonance & Asteri Rules

- A card belongs visually to its printed Resonance first, even when its subject has history in another faction.
- Cross-Resonance history is shown through **small inherited details**, not by splitting the palette 50/50. Example: Varkesh remains visually Fracture, but one faded Dawnline stripe proves his Beacon past.
- Asteri objects use a neutral ancient language: pale stone + black glass + aged metal + calm impossible geometry. Resonance colors should appear only when the object is currently reacting.
- The Silence / Zero Resonance should be represented primarily through **absence**: muted material response, swallowed reflections, no aura, no particles.

**Role:** Keeper of the Atlas; present-day protagonist

**Source-derived identity:** Lore: explorer of Asteri ruins who discovers a Living Atlas Fragment in 317 AS.

# 6. Landmark Visual Anchor Sheets
## Whisperglass Relay

**Location:** Glassward Coast near Rift Sea
**Visual anchor:** Ancient relay tower complex of smoked glass, mirrorsteel bridges, suspended reflective pylons; still operational when sister relays failed.
**Gameplay memory:** Echo Assault
**Production note:** Must visually imply continued motion / repeated advance, not a static tower.

## Moonbridge Crossing

**Location:** Across the Shattered Meridian
**Visual anchor:** Elegant impossible bridge with missing physical spans replaced by moonlit Glasswind continuity; some arches exist in different positions depending on viewpoint.
**Gameplay memory:** Resonant refund
**Production note:** Must feel like part of the route was never paid/never traversed; avoid generic magical bridge.

## Starfall Observatory

**Location:** Astral Reach
**Visual anchor:** Asteri observatory of pale stone and black glass, all major instruments oriented toward one starless region of sky.
**Gameplay memory:** Foresee
**Production note:** Dark Interval must be visible as intentional absence, not ordinary night sky.

## Stillwater Sanctuary

**Location:** Stillwater Basin
**Visual anchor:** Quiet sanctuary built into a perfectly calm lake, reflecting earlier ideal states rather than simple mirror images.
**Gameplay memory:** Renewal
**Production note:** Water is the hero. Avoid spa / temple cliché.

## Forge of Echoes

**Location:** Ashen Marches near Meridian
**Visual anchor:** Ancient forge with no conventional fire; remembered ideal states appear as ghosted material overlays around objects.
**Gameplay memory:** Tempered Arrival
**Production note:** Heat source should be memory-light, not lava.

## Hall of Returning Stars

**Location:** Variable / Returning Reach
**Visual anchor:** Impossible Asteri hall whose doors and stairs connect eras and locations; architecture remains elegant and ceremonial.
**Gameplay memory:** Swift Arrival
**Production note:** Distinguish from GS1 Hall of Returning Steps: Stars is the larger Landmark complex; Steps is a specific inner passage.

- [ ] Hand crop preserves identity and primary action

- [ ] image depicts the card's actual lore/mechanic identity rather than generic fantasy

- Place all 48 thumbnails on one contact sheet and verify that no two neighboring cards are visually interchangeable.
